# Surface brief: app shell and all product routes

Scope: every authenticated route plus auth pages. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: the owner and a small invited circle running active job hunts; returning daily to move applications forward and write evidence once.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md principles; progressive enhancement; owner-scoped data; scores always carry the heuristic disclaimer.
Chosen direction: Exhibit Binder (user choice on the decision page, 2026-10-21). Memorable moment: every claim carries an exhibit citation back to its evidence.
Unresolved: dark theme deferred (light only for now).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The job hunt as a well-kept exhibit binder. Evidence is filed once as numbered exhibits; every document and score cites them. Refuses the category default of a dark SaaS dashboard with neon accent and card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-gray binder board ground; one white sheet per page carrying the work; ruled hairlines instead of nested panels; index-tab navigation whose active tab joins the sheet; rubber-stamp status marks in colored ink; "Ex. N" citation chips; exhibit-blue (#24427a) for actions, manila for exhibit labels, margin red for destructive and for the writing sheet's double margin rule. Libre Franklin for UI, Source Serif 4 for writing, mono only for code.

STORY: The user sees what is filed, what cites what, and what to do next; they trust generated documents because each line points at an exhibit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Board-colored top band with the wordmark left and index tabs (Find jobs, Applications, Tailor, Exhibits, Profile) across; the active tab merges into the white sheet beneath, which holds the page title, a one-line status, and the primary action at the title's right.

FORM: Exhibit Binder, candidate 2 on the grounded list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen over the assigned Herbarium Sheet); seed key 7800247e; roll ran degraded (no challengers); code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
